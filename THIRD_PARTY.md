# Third-party notices

The project uses Godot as its selected engine. No engine binaries, external plugins, code libraries or production asset packs are currently distributed in this repository.

Before adding a dependency or distributing a build, record its name, version, source, license and required notices. Godot's engine terms remain separate from the game's rights notice. A restrictive project notice does not replace third-party permissions.

Production artwork and audio are tracked in [asset provenance](assets/provenance.md). Workflow actions are pinned in .github/workflows/ and retain their respective upstream terms.
