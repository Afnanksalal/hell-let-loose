# Design brief

The game follows customized babies who live with their families by day and secretly protect their neighborhood at night. Friends socialize, care for themselves, explore and cause trouble before escaping their cribs to face supernatural incidents.

Daytime choices affect equipment, abilities, story opportunities and household suspicion. Nighttime outcomes affect the next day. Tasks emerge from the story and world state rather than a fixed checklist.

The target is 2 to 8 cooperative players and sessions of roughly 20 to 30 minutes. Tactical move-based battles, extensive customization, comic narration and colorful expressive cartoon art are central requirements. Exact combat pacing, runtime technology and content scope remain open.

Across the campaign, characters grow toward adulthood and gradually forget their identity. Their generation reaches an ending and new babies emerge. The final aging rules and sequel boundary are undecided.

The repository will contain the full game implementation and assets as development proceeds. Its material is public for viewing and study under the rights notice in [LICENSE](../LICENSE).
