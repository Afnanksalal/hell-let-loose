# Episode examples

All examples are assistant proposals, not canon. [Sources](../../sources/README.md).

## The Woman in the Window

Earlier 25-minute example for six players: a baby sees a woman whom adults cannot see. Friends find an old photo of her. One steals a necklace; another breaks a vase. Suspicion makes sunset escape harder and teammates distract parents. In an abandoned house, the woman's spirit searches for her child. Possessed furniture and a nursery puzzle reveal a controlling demon. A ritual uses and destroys the necklace. At dawn, missing jewelry and broken evidence may result in a babysitter next day.

The useful principle is the causal chain, not mandatory minutes, party size or item destruction.

## The Babysitter Who Never Blinks

A suspiciously perfect sitter confiscates favorite toys. A miniature heist finds a 1974 photograph containing the identical sitter. At night a cupboard unfolds into an impossible nursery; confiscated toys become her battle squad. Rescuing toys during the day changes the fight. Afterward the parents book her every Friday.

The sitter, date and plot are inventions in the current assistant brainstorm, not a selected first mission.

## The stolen necklace branch

Return a glowing necklace to prevent tracking, investigate for a hidden objective or keep it for danger and possible reward. This earlier example illustrates branching consequences, not a required quest.
