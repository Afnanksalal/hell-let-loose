# Episode studies

These drafts test the connection between daytime choices, night encounters and next-day consequences. Neither is selected for production.

## The Woman in the Window

A baby sees a woman outside the nursery. The parent sees nobody. An old photograph shows her in the same place decades earlier.

Friends gather clues while someone takes a silver necklace and another breaks a vase. The breakage raises suspicion and makes that household harder to escape. Teammates return to distract the parents.

In an abandoned house, the group discovers a spirit searching for her child. Possessed furniture and a nursery puzzle reveal a controlling demon. The necklace can weaken it, at the cost of destroying the item.

The group succeeds but returns to missing jewelry and broken evidence. Extra supervision the next day becomes a consequence of the choices that helped win the night.

## The Babysitter Who Never Blinks

A new sitter confiscates the babies' favorite toys. A small heist finds a photograph from 1974 with the same sitter, unchanged.

At night, a cupboard opens into an impossible nursery. The confiscated toys fight for her. Rescuing a toy during the day changes what the group faces; leaving a drum behind gives her a percussion attack.

Afterward, the parents are delighted that she is available every Friday. That leaves both a joke and a reason for the story to continue.
