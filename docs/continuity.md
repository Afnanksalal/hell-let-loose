# Linear continuity

Sources: [chat one](../sources/chat-01.md), [chat two](../sources/chat-02.md), [latest direction](../sources/current-conversation.md).

## Session

1. Create and deeply personalize a baby; join friends in the neighborhood.
2. Day: care, socialize, explore, investigate and cause mischief through dynamic situations.
3. Carry decisions forward: needs, relationships, items and behavior influence powers, opportunities and complications.
4. Sunset: escape household supervision, help teammates and assemble dramatically.
5. Night: investigate the supernatural story, solve cooperative objectives and undertake Pokémon-inspired battles.
6. Dawn: return, hide evidence and resolve rewards and consequences.
7. Next day: altered households, relationships and mysteries continue the story.

## Campaign

Repeated connected sessions develop characters, customization, equipment, friendships and the main mystery. Maps expand and difficulty increases. The [earlier five-chapter outline](narrative/world-and-campaign.md) remains a proposal, not final canon.

## Lifetime

Grow from babies through childhood and adolescence toward 18/adulthood. Identity gradually fades. The original generation reaches a narrative ending; new babies emerge. Whether they appear in game one, postgame or a sequel is open.

A 25-minute session is not an entire childhood. Aging pace and triggers are undecided. Characters do not become adult warriors every sunset. The earlier “babies remain babies” recommendation cannot override the user's later lifetime progression.
