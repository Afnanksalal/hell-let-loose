# Session and campaign sequence

## One session

1. Join friends with a customized character.
2. Spend the day eating, resting, socializing, exploring and getting into trouble.
3. Carry useful objects, clues, relationships and behavior effects into bedtime.
4. Escape the household and help anyone who is still stuck inside.
5. Meet the group, investigate the night's incident and complete its battles or other objectives.
6. Return before morning and deal with evidence of the escape.
7. Begin the next day with changed household conditions and story consequences.

## The campaign

Connected sessions develop relationships, equipment and the neighborhood mystery. New locations and stronger threats appear as the story advances. Failure can introduce a complication rather than erase the session's progress.

## Growing up

Aging spans the campaign, not the minutes of a single match. The group grows through childhood and adolescence toward adulthood. Memories and identity fade, bringing the original generation to its endgame. New babies emerge afterward.

Progression triggers, mixed-age parties and the placement of the next generation are recorded as open decisions in the [decision register](../decisions/register.md).
